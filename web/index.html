Quiet by design

Built to stay out of the way

A background app costs you battery through wakeups, not work. Almost everything here arrives as a system notification instead of being polled.

One timer, not many

The clipboard is the only thing macOS gives no notification for. It is checked on an interval that stretches when the hub is closed or you are on battery, with a tolerance so the wakeup rides along with others.

Steps down on battery

Low Power Mode, thermal pressure and unplugging are all watched. Motion stops, blur is dropped, polling relaxes — automatically.

No surprises at login

Opening at login is off until you ask, and registered the way macOS expects, so it appears in System Settings where you can overrule it.

Privacy

Your Mac, and only your Mac

No account, no server, no analytics in the app.

Clipboard is encrypted

History is stored encrypted under a key from your keychain. Copies from apps you exclude are never recorded, and anything a password manager marks concealed is skipped.

Licences verify offline

The app carries only a public key: it can check a licence is genuine without ever phoning home. It works on a plane, and years from now.

Permissions only when used

Nothing is requested at launch. Calendar, Automation and Accessibility are each asked for the first time you use the feature that needs them.

Questions

Before you download

Does it work without a notch?

Yes. On a Mac without one the hub uses the menu bar height instead and behaves the same. Hiding the notch obviously does nothing there.

What permissions does it ask for, and when?

None at launch. Controlling Music or Spotify asks for Automation the first time you press play. YouTube Music uses the media keys, which needs Accessibility. The Day tab asks for Calendar. Everything else needs nothing, and each feature explains itself before the system prompt appears.

Why does YouTube Music show less than Spotify?

Because it is a web page rather than an app, it publishes no track information. Jendela. reads the browser tab title, which gives the song. For cover art and true play state it also needs Chrome's View → Developer → Allow JavaScript from Apple Events, and the app tells you so rather than quietly showing nothing.

Does the Discord tab connect to Discord?

Not for call state. Discord only exposes that through its RPC interface, which requires a registered application and your authorisation, so the overlay's camera and share switches are set by you. Whether Discord is running is detected properly.

Is my clipboard sent anywhere?

No. It never leaves the Mac and is stored encrypted. There is no sync, no account and no analytics.

What happens when the trial ends?

The hub, notes, music, sound and hiding the notch keep working permanently. Clipboard history shortens to five entries rather than disappearing, and the shelf, Day, Ask and widgets ask for a licence. Nothing you wrote is taken away.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon or Intel.
